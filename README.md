A tiny Python program that writes a single excuse line to
`/output/entry.txt`. The line is told by the dog himself, caught in the
act, and it runs on pure dog logic: the homework smelled of his human's
hands and stress, so eating it was protection work -- and, as a bonus, he
marked the math while he chewed. No guilt, no apology, just a dog who is
proud of a job well done.

## The entry

> It smelled like your hands and pure stress, so I ate it before it could hurt you. I protect this family. Also, your math was wrong. I checked while chewing.

(156 characters)

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
